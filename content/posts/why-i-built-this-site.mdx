For years my work lived on other people's platforms. LinkedIn for the job stuff. Dev.to for writing. GitHub for code. Instagram for the fun stuff.

That works until someone searches your name and gets a scattered mess.

## So this is home now

Everything starts here. Posts go up on this site first, then get cross-posted to Dev.to with a canonical link pointing back. Same words, one source of truth.

## What I'll write about

- Micro-frontends and Module Federation, the parts the tutorials skip
- State management that doesn't fight you
- How popular apps work under the hood
- Things I break while building side projects

If any of that sounds useful, check back every few weeks. Or just say hi.
